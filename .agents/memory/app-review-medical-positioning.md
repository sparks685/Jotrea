---
name: App Review medical positioning
description: Durable copy and product constraint for keeping Jotrea within Apple's tracker/reminder classification.
---

Jotrea must always be presented as a personal tracker/reminder for medication, dosage, and frequency already prescribed by a healthcare provider. It must never appear to calculate, recommend, modify, verify, or establish a dosage or medical plan.

Do not render catalog medication instructions as personalized guidance. Avoid in-app missed-dose, escalation, administration, timing, interaction, or storage directives; send users to their prescription, healthcare provider/pharmacist, and official Sources & References instead. Custom medication dosage and frequency must be explicitly entered from the prescription and must not have a suggested default.

**Why:** Apple rejected Jotrea under Guideline 1.4.2 after wording such as “Building your dose schedule” and “Building your plan” made the app appear to generate a medical or dosage plan.

**How to apply:** Audit all new user-facing medication, onboarding, dashboard, logging, Settings, Med Info, marketing, and App Store copy for tracker/reminder framing before shipping. Preserve explicit disclaimers that Jotrea does not calculate or recommend dosages.
