name: Jotrea Plus model
description: Approved free-versus-paid product boundaries and launch pricing for Jotrea Plus.
---

Keep basic medication tracking, complete in-app history, weight and symptom logging, and basic reminders free. Jotrea Plus adds external PDF/CSV export, multiple medications, a Medication Cabinet with additional reminders, Apple Health/HealthKit sync, private Visit Notes, and provider visit summaries. Advanced Trends is intentionally excluded from v1.1 until it materially exceeds the free charts.

Launch pricing is $4.99 monthly or $39.99 annually, with a 14-day free trial.

**Why:** The free core builds trust and preserves access to essential personal records inside Jotrea, while Plus charges for private visit organization, external sharing, native integrations, and higher-value reporting.

**How to apply:** Use these boundaries for RevenueCat entitlements, paywalls, App Store products, screenshots, and marketing copy. Never restrict in-app history; gate every PDF/CSV export at tap time while leaving its button visible.
