name: Jotrea Plus model
description: Approved free-versus-paid product boundaries and launch pricing for Jotrea Plus.
---

Keep basic medication tracking, history, weight and symptom logging, basic reminders, and basic export free. Jotrea Plus should add multiple medications, a Medication Cabinet with additional reminders, Apple Health/HealthKit sync, advanced trends, and provider visit summaries.

Launch pricing is $4.99 monthly or $39.99 annually, with a 14-day free trial.

**Why:** The free core builds trust and preserves access to essential personal records, while Plus charges for native integrations, expanded organization, and higher-value reporting.

**How to apply:** Use these boundaries when implementing RevenueCat entitlements, paywalls, App Store subscription products, screenshots, and marketing copy. Do not move basic history or basic export behind the paywall.
