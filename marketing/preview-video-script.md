**Aspect ratio:** 9:16 (1290×2796)

## Production Notes

- **No voiceover** — music only, let the UI speak
- **All transitions:** smooth slide/fade, match app's Framer Motion feel
- **Taps:** show finger/cursor indicator for clarity
- **Music mood:** calm, warm, intentional — not energetic or pushy
- **Color grade:** preserve cream/terracotta palette, no filters
- **Text font:** Inter, matching app typography exactly
- **Export:** H.264, 1290×2796, 60fps, < 500MB

## Suggested Tools
- Screen recording: iOS built-in or QuickTime
- Editing: DaVinci Resolve or CapCut
- Title cards: add in editor with Inter font, white on black background
