## Description (4000 chars max)

**Jotrea — Jot your dose. Read your progress.**

Starting a GLP-1 medication like Ozempic, Mounjaro, Wegovy, or Zepbound is a big step. Jotrea helps you stay on track with every injection, see your weight progress clearly, and understand your medication — all in one calm, beautifully designed app.

**TRACK EVERY DOSE**
Never wonder "did I take my injection this week?" again. Jotrea shows a clear countdown to the next dose on the schedule you entered, makes logging as easy as one tap, and records injection sites so you can review your history.

**SEE YOUR WEIGHT STORY**
Weigh in after each dose and watch your progress unfold on a gorgeous trend chart. Jotrea calculates your total weight lost, weekly average, BMI, and how close you are to your goal. Celebrate every milestone — even the small ones.

**KEEP YOUR PRESCRIBED INFORMATION HANDY**
Record the medication, dosage, and frequency from your prescription, then keep your dose history and reminders together. Jotrea helps you track the plan provided by your healthcare professional; it never calculates or recommends a dosage.

**STAY CONSISTENT WITH STREAKS**
Your weekly streak counter keeps you motivated. Four weeks on track? Eight weeks? Jotrea sees every dose and celebrates your consistency.
