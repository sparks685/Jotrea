# Jotrea — Brand Narrative

---

## The Name

**Jotrea** is built from two words that define the entire product:

- **Jot** — the act of logging, recording, marking. A jot is small but deliberate. It's what you do when something matters: you write it down. In Jotrea, a "jot" is every dose you log, every weigh-in you record, every note you leave for yourself. Simple. Intentional. Yours.

- **Rea** — short for *reading*, as in reading your progress. Not just glancing — truly reading the story your body is telling. The weight trend chart, the streak counter, the countdown ring — these are pages in your health narrative. Jotrea helps you read them clearly.

Together: **Jot your dose. Read your progress.**

This isn't just a tagline. It's the product in six words.

---

## Brand Voice

**Simple.** No jargon. No medical overwhelm. If a sentence needs to be re-read, it's too long.

**Calm.** GLP-1 journeys can be stressful — doctors, dosing schedules, side effects, slow progress. Jotrea is the still point in that storm. Warm tones, soft edges, unhurried language.

**Empowering.** The user is in control of their journey. Jotrea is their tool, not their boss. We celebrate consistency, not perfection. Missing a dose isn't a failure — it's a data point.

**Warm.** Not clinical. Not gym-bro. Not diet-culture. We speak to real people doing something genuinely hard and genuinely hopeful.

## Brand Personality

If Jotrea were a person, they would be:
- A knowledgeable friend who happens to be a nurse practitioner
- Always calm, never alarmist
- Organized but not rigid
- Celebrates small wins genuinely
- Doesn't lecture you when you miss a dose
- Knows when to give you data and when to give you encouragement

---

## What Jotrea Is NOT

- Not a diet app (no calorie counting, no food shaming)
- Not a fitness tracker (no step goals, no workout logs)
- Not a medical app (no diagnoses, no prescriptions)
- Not a social app (no sharing, no leaderboards, no comparisons)
- Not overwhelming (never more information than you need right now)

---

## Design Principles

1. **One action per screen** — the dashboard has one primary CTA. Always.
2. **Data celebrates, never judges** — weight going up? Jotrea shows it without comment.
3. **Offline first** — works without internet. Your health data doesn't need a server.
4. **Native feel** — smooth spring animations, bottom sheets, haptic-ready interactions.
5. **Premium earns its keep** — free tier is genuinely useful. Premium unlocks real value, not fake limitations.
