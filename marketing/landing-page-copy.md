## SECTION 4 — FAQ

**Q: Which GLP-1 medications does Jotrea support?**
A: Jotrea supports 11 medications: Ozempic (semaglutide injection), Mounjaro (tirzepatide), Wegovy (semaglutide higher dose), Zepbound (tirzepatide), Rybelsus (semaglutide oral), Victoza, Saxenda, Trulicity, Bydureon, Byetta, and Adlyxin.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes. Jotrea stores everything locally on your device using your iPhone's secure storage. No account is required. No data is sent to a server. Your health information stays entirely yours.

**Q: Does Jotrea work offline?**
A: Completely. Jotrea never requires internet access. Log doses, track weight, and read medication info anywhere — no Wi-Fi, no cellular needed.

**Q: Is Jotrea a medical app?**
A: No. Jotrea is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device or clinical application. It doesn't provide medical advice, diagnoses, or treatment recommendations. Always follow your healthcare provider's instructions.

**Q: Can I use Jotrea for daily medications like Rybelsus?**
A: Yes. Jotrea supports weekly, daily, and twice-daily dosing schedules. The countdown ring and calendar adjust automatically to your medication's frequency.

**Q: What happens if I miss a dose?**
A: Jotrea marks it in your calendar history without judgment. Missed doses are just data — they don't affect your streak going forward, and they help you see patterns over time.
